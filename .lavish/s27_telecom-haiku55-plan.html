What the fresh draw does to v1's tasks

7 of v1's 20 train tasks go to test, and 15 of its 20 test tasks go to train. v2 starts from v1's prompt, which an optimiser wrote after reading 6 failed v0 train conversations. 2 of those 6 tasks now sit in test, so v2's test may read slightly high on those 2 (marked below). Those are A's numbers. Under B, 8 go to test, 13 go to train, and 3 of the 6 sit in test.

04 · The runs — 114 conversations, about 2–3 hours if no limit hits

  1. 1

    Smoke: 6 train tasks, 2 per symptom

    Telecom has never been played under sim rules tau2_loop/2 (the customer check, the 2025-02-25 clock note), nor by Haiku 5.5 in json tool mode. Checks: 0 infrastructure errors, how many customer turns were sent back, and whether every reply parses. The ids go to the CLI quoted: through make … TASKS=, the | in a telecom id would reach the shell as a pipe.

    uv run tau2loop eval --domain telecom --agent v2 --split train \
  --task '<id 1>' … --task '<id 6>' \
  --note "s27 smoke"
  2. 2

    Train: extend the smoke to all 74

    This plays only the other 68 and joins them to the smoke as one run of the split, as v8's train run was made. No task is played twice.

    make extend RUN=<smoke run> NOTE="s27 train"
  3. 3

    Test: all 40, alongside step 2

    make eval DOMAIN=telecom AGENT=v2 SPLIT=test NOTE="s27 test"
  4. 4

    Read: infrastructure errors first, then the score

    Pass counts with their denominators, by symptom and by persona, with the failed tasks listed. A session limit is waited out and never restarted.

Customer flags depend on D1: USER_MODEL=claude-haiku-5-5 USER_EFFORT=high under B, nothing under A, where it becomes telecom's default.

05 · Promotion — v2 becomes champion as a model swap; v1 is not replayed

what runsmake promote, kind "model swap"

make promote RUN=<v2 train run> KIND="model swap" points loop/telecom/registry.json at v2's 74-task train run and appends a promote event of that kind. It also moves the MLflow alias champion on tau2-loop-telecom and registers v2's prompt.

why not make challengeit would play v1 on the 74 first

The gate compares a challenger with the champion on the same tasks. loop.run._covers finds no v1 run of them, so v1 would be played again, which you ruled out. There is precedent: banking v1 was promoted as a model swap, and v3 and v8 as tool changes.

what CI checksthe gate re-scores the 74 offline

make gate (tracking/gate.py) checks v2's fingerprint and pass count. It also replays the champion's saved conversations through tau2's evaluators, now 74 telecom conversations instead of 20. I run it locally before any push.

v2 is promoted whatever it scores, provided all 114 conversations are scored with 0 infrastructure errors. If not, I stop and report.

The registry will read v1 19/20 → v2 n/74. The two numbers are not comparable: the tasks, the customer and the sim rules all differ. CLAUDE.md will say so in telecom's champion line.
