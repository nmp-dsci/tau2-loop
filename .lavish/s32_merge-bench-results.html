06 · Caveats — one trial each, one grade each, and nothing played yet for D-lean

  1. One trial each. s30's plan was a second trial for the two leaders (O8). Neither B t2 nor a D-lean t2 has run, so 8.0 against 7.5 is one fact on one grade each, not a measured gap.
  2. The grader is Opus, at 20 of 22 hand labels. Sonnet 5.5 agreed on 19 of 22 and called r19 v3's missing K8 right, so it does not grade these results (O8's condition). Its replies are cached under their own name and used nowhere.
  3. The answer key measures the job, not the conversation. E5 plays it. v28 reading B's job passed 1 of 5 train conversations against v27's 0 and got 74 of 110 gold fields against 83 (s30). D-lean's job has not been played.
  4. K4 is wrong in both leaders. Both readings of provisional credit give a different answer from gold on cases the key names. The merge kept the wrong reading because the documents allow it. The fix is a reading rule in workflow_rag, as a new rN, not a merge option.
  5. Correction to s31. r19 v3, the job v27 reads, scores 5 of 9 with the calibrated grader, not 5.5. 5.5 was the first, uncalibrated pass. s30's other baselines move the same way: r18 v1 6, r19 v1 5.5, r19 v2 6.
