Research · s08 · leaderboard, 25 agent designs, prompt optimisers and noise · airline

Airline's best stock agent scores 84.0% pass^1, and harnesses that check write calls 87–88.5%, on 50 tasks × 4 trials — tau2-loop tunes only the prompt, on one trial

Three research passes read about 60 sources: the τ²-bench leaderboard and lab write-ups, 25 agent designs with measured results, and the prompt-optimiser and evaluation-noise literature. The stock agent tops out at 84.0% pass^1 (Claude Opus 4.5, effort high, GPT-5.2 as user). Custom harnesses that check write calls report 87.0–88.5%. Your Sonnet 5 champion scores 21/25 on train and 21/25 on test, at one trial with a Haiku user: close, but not comparable.

What this page is for

Most of the gap between your loop and the best airline agents is in two places your optimiser cannot reach: measurement (one trial, 25 tasks) and the harness (nothing checks a write call before it runs). Section 6 ranks 15 changes for this repo; pick a disposition for each and queue them.

On the user simulator

In a comparison of 31 simulators against 451 real people, Claude Haiku 4.5 scored 63.2 on realism against a best of 76.0 (humans 92.7). Claude Sonnet 4 (62.0) and Opus 4 (59.2) were no better. So a larger Claude as the user is not an obvious fix. Tagging failures that are the simulator's fault is: in cycle 4, the Haiku user leaked the real date and quit task 19. Sim2Real, arXiv 2603.11245

4 · The optimiser — whole-prompt rewrites overfit 25 tasks

No paper optimises a Claude agent's prompt on τ²-airline with real user simulation and a proper held-out split. The closest work agrees on three things. Validate on tasks the optimiser didn't read. Edit in small, itemised deltas. Reject regressions during the search, not after it.
