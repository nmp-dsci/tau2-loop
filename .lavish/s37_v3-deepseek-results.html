08 · Caveats — a Claude customer, two changes at once, one trial

  1. Can these conversations train a student? Not in tau-telecom-sft. Its rule 2 takes agent turns from open-weights models only and reads a conversation against a Claude customer as not trainable; every customer turn here is Haiku 5.5's. Step one measured accuracy; the teacher pass needs a customer that isn't Claude.
  2. Is the comparison like for like? Same tasks (split v3, seed 300), same customer (Haiku 5.5 at high), same rules (tau2_loop/2), same prompt text. Two things changed together: the model and the tool mode, since v2 wrote its calls as JSON and v3 calls tools natively. The gain belongs to the pair, not to DeepSeek alone.
  3. How sure is the number? One trial a task. Over both splits the 30–7 split of changed tasks gives p = 0.0002; test alone gives p = 0.065. v2's runs are nine days older, with the same customer settings.
  4. Does the one-call rule hide anything? It dropped 428 extra calls and the text beside 221 calls across 114 conversations. In the 22 failures it cost no gold step: the drops never re-issued were lookups of another line and a plan, and in both words-only transfers DeepSeek's own reply had no call. The 92 passes were not read.
