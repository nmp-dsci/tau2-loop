05 · The prompt line: "disputed_amount = the full transaction" has been wrong since v24, and r18's workflow hid it

- Debit dispute fields come from the customer's words: discovery_date = when they first noticed; disputed_amount = the full transaction; transaction_type from how the card was used. customer_max_liability_amount is a Regulation E tier, a maximum: 50 when reported within 2 business days of the statement showing the charge (a charge since the last statement has none yet, so it is in time); 500 only when the customer is reporting a charge from an older statement late; -1 after 60 days. pin_compromised from their words (no when they used it normally or say it is safe); transaction_type is doc 031's: pin_purchase an in-store purchase with PIN, signature_purchase one with signature; take it from what the customer or the record says, and ask when neither does. Fraud, unauthorized, ATM cash and duplicate need no merchant contact.
