04 · Why — the write turns are half the cost, and a fifth went to merges that must be redone

  1. The job is written out in full, twice. Every merge turn returns the whole job as JSON (97K to 132K characters, 42K to 60K output tokens), and the one send-back returns it all again, even when it names three steps. 21 merge replies wrote 2.70M characters. the write turns: 48% of weighted cost, 54% of output
  2. The job grows with each merge. B's job went from 84,563 to 123,888 characters in four merges (+46%), D's to 126,774 in one. Every later write, read, review and grade grows with it, and so does the answering agent's reading: v28 read 0.96M to 1.48M tokens a conversation. compounds every other cost
  3. The merge runs at the end of the research conversation. A merge turn re-reads the replayed research, 50 to 89 messages: 150K to 330K tokens a call. The cache served 47% of the bench's input. The audit's text sits after the cache marks, so B's first merge turn on 088 read 184K tokens with 6% cached. most of the 9.82M input
  4. Merge turns search again. 16 merge turns called a tool before writing. Each one re-sent the whole conversation: 150K to 240K tokens for one search result, 3.2M input in all (32% of the bench's input). 10% of weighted cost
  5. The reviewer reads every job, blocked ones included. The reviewer (D14) made 52 calls, 15% of the cost. The replayed draft for 088 and 090 holds 5 jobs; the merge writes all 5, the 4 it does not hold are blocked, and the reviewer still reads them. 14% of the merge turns' output (369K of 2.70M characters) went to jobs that cannot be taken. reviewer 15%; blocked jobs about 4%
  6. C adds three stages and none of its merges went in. Extract, verify and write are fresh contexts with 50K to 92K input and 57K to 81K output each: 247K to 278K output per merge, against 150K to 190K for A and B. The binding reviewer then refused all three of C's finished merges (D20). C: 32% of the cost, no merge taken
  7. Crashes, the pause and high effort. The D10 crash cost A's and B's first 090 merges (0.93M input, 227K output, redone), and the pause loses the three merges in flight (0.96M input, 411K output). Every merge and stage also runs Opus at high effort, about 10K thinking tokens a write. 21% of the cost so far goes to merges that are redone

Shares are this page's estimates from Fig 2–4, not measured separately. Not counted: this Claude Code session itself, which re-reads its own long context every turn and is billed to the same subscription.
