06 · The rule — a gap in the documents is not permission, in both audits and in the merge

audit.md · a fifth kind of clauseWhat both audits may no longer strike

- kept: a clause that forbids an action (an unlock, a reset, a closure,
  a transfer, any write) or holds back an offer, where no passage decides
  the case. A gap in the documents is not permission: the clause stays.
  A passage overrules it only when the passage names this case itself
  (its field value, code, product or condition), not when it reaches the
  case only through a category or heading the case falls under ("any
  automatic trigger", "scores above thresholds"). Then the clause is
  unsupported and is rewritten to that passage.

valid: every deciding clause is stated, derived, a question or kept.

merge.md · rule 1's last sentenceWhat the merge does when nothing decides

1. The document decides, whoever wrote the step. [...] When no passage
   decides it, keep the instruction that does less (no write, no unlock,
   no offer of one) and list the case in `open_questions`. A step asks
   the customer only for a fact the case turns on, never whether they
   want an action that no passage names for the case.
  • What it fixes. In both trials the first audit struck "do not reset or otherwise act on it" from the security-hold step, because doc 041's Step 6 covers "automatic escalation triggers", a category the hold falls under. The merge and the second audit then let the rewrite stand. Only the reset tool's code says a reset unlocks the card. Trial 2's second audit struck "if they say no, do not unlock" from the 5–7 question in the same way.
  • What it costs the debit job. Only one of D's 27 rewrites there loosened a write, and it did so with a passage that names the case: doc 025, the card's own pending transactions. The rule still allows that, so it should leave the 9.0 alone. Step 6 checks this on r20's own version.
  • What it does not fix. Rules that only gold decides: the suspicious window (K3), the card order (K5), and T4 on the debit job.
  • Within the rules for workflow_rag. It is a general reading rule that names no case, no task and no gold answer.
