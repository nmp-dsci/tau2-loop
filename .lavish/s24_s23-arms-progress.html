07 · Harness — the weekly limit wrote 34 errors as failures

What happened

Every v25 conversation returned "You've hit your weekly limit · resets Oct 15 at 12pm". The runner recorded each as infrastructure_error, but run.json says n_scored: 17, passed: 0. The viewer would show v25 at 0 of 17.

Why

llm/core.py:378 waits out a "session limit" message until its window resets. A "weekly limit" message is not matched, so it fails straight through as an error.

Also fixed this cycle

The first optimiser session ran git add and git commit itself. It was rejected and undone, and guards.bash_fence_reason now refuses git (7c2c8f0).

The two v25 folders hold no conversation. They are not committed, and their fate is decision C in §09.
