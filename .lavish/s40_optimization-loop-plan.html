How the lookup works: workflows/library.py rank() scores r20's 53 jobs by the words the agent's one-line request shares with each job's name, trigger and steps. Rarer words weigh more, and a word in the job's name counts twice. It lists 8 and shows the best match in full. No model is called, so the same request always gets the same answer. "Own job" means a job that a workflow_rag session researching this very question wrote or merged into, in any rN from r1 to r20. The library hands the agent the right procedure. What fails is inside the procedure, or in how much the agent trusts it.

  1. Is test unseen by the library? No. All 37 test questions were researched from their customer scripts in r3 (8 Oct, your call), and 10 of them again in r18–r20. 37 of r20's 53 jobs hold test research, and 9 hold only test research. 7 of the 37 test conversations rely on those 9 jobs alone, and 5 of the 7 passed. A truly unseen question would get no such step, so test overstates how the library does on a new question.
  2. Is it overfitting? Partly. From v4 to v30, train failures fell from 31 to 11 (20 of 31 fixed) and test failures from 23 to 13 (10 of 23). Every optimiser reads train failures, and the s22 defect loop (r5–r17) ran 13 sessions on train questions and 5 on test ones.
  3. Is it noise? Some of it. One trial gives Fisher p = 0.09. Between v30's 22 Oct and 26 Oct runs, 6 of the 34 hardest tasks changed result, though the customer changed between those runs too.
  4. What can close it? The job-step fixes reach test more than train: 3 of the 5 narrowing failures (task_015, task_026, task_077) and the dropped branch (task_014) are test. The gold flaws stay, and they leave 6.8 points between the ceilings (56 of 60 against 32 of 37).

About 7 of the 17 points are gold flaws that no fix can close. The job-step fixes aim at the other 7.

07 · Sonnet 5.5 or Opus — a model can reach 10 failures at most

Fig 9 · could a stronger answering model, or Sonnet 5.5 at higher effort, fix it? The 24 failures

  1. The ceiling of a swap. Even if Opus fixed all 10 reachable failures, v30 would reach 53 of 60 on train and 30 of 37 on test. The fixes in §06 aim at the same 10, plus the 3 rounding ones.
  2. What we know. No banking answering version has ever run on Opus: 22 ran on Sonnet 5 and 8 on Sonnet 5.5, and 27 of the 30 at medium effort. Sonnet 5 to 5.5 (v8 to v23, with other changes and another customer) moved train from 38 to 43 of 60 and test from 21 to 20 of 37. Opus already does the deep reading where it pays: workflow_rag's research and merges (r20's stages run Opus at high).
  3. The cost. Opus uses more of the subscription and plays slower, and v30's Sonnet test run already took 3 h 13 min for 37 conversations. A usage limit voided v25 on 15 Oct.
  4. The probe. v31 = v30 on claude-opus-5-5 at medium, and v32 = v30 on Sonnet 5.5 at high. Each plays the 10 reachable failures twice and 10 passing tasks once: 30 conversations each, 60 in all. Switch only if Opus fixes at least 4 of 10 in both trials and breaks none of the 10 passes. 6 of the 10 are test conversations, which follows your practice since s25. The gate still reads the full test run.

If Opus also follows the wrong steps, that proves the bottleneck is the library. If it overrides them, G1 is the cheaper way to get the same effect.

  4. 03

    G2 and G3 as rubric code checks, then re-merge every job they flag (expected: debit dispute, cash back, referral link, card replacement, fee credits). Targets task_073, task_086, task_015, task_026, task_077, task_014. Re-play every conversation shown a changed job. Expected +2 to +5, most of it on test.

  5. 04

    Bundle D · split the big jobs (r23)after C

    The 8 jobs with 40 or more steps split by their deciding fact. No failure is targeted; measure pass^2 and the own-job rate (must stay 96 of 97).

  6. 05

    Alongside · the Opus probe§09 Q2

    §07: v31-opus and v32-high on the 10 reachable failures plus 10 passes. 60 conversations. Independent of the bundles.

Every bundle is scored like for like with v30: the Sonnet 5.5 customer at medium, the Haiku 5.5 judge and tau2_loop/2. Its target failures play first, twice each. Then comes every train conversation whose job changed, to catch breakage, then all of train, then test once. Promotion stays your call, or the gate's test McNemar. Each change is a general reading rule or a code check, each rN has its changes.json, and each step is committed and reported before the next.

If A to C land as expected, train goes from 49 to 53–56 of 60 and test from 24 to 29–32 of 37. A run that follows the documents tops out at 56 and 32.
