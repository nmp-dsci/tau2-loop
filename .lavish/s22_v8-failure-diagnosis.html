07 · Plan — r5 reads the documents strictly; code checks two rules

r5 is written and tested, not run: r4's cache and edits fixes, five new reading rules in its research prompt, and two new checks in its merge gate. A merge that fails a check is sent back once with what to fix, like D8.

The five reading rules, as r5's prompt states them

  1. Trace each write's requirements. Open the procedure document of the tool the write calls and list every requirement it sets: a status, an age, a balance, a limit, an earlier step. For each, ask whether this customer's situation can fail it, counting any state an earlier step of this job, or of a job the customer may do first, can leave behind. When it can, search the documents for a procedure whose result meets the requirement, read that procedure's own requirements too, and write its steps before the write. When no procedure meets it, write the step that stops and tells the customer what must be resolved. Quote each requirement and each procedure you chain.

How r5 reaches unfreeze-then-cancel without being told

Neither r5's prompt nor its code mentions freezing, unfreezing or closing a card. Two general mechanisms get there from the documents.

  1. The research step (rule 1). For the close, it opens the closing procedure and lists its requirements, among them “must currently be in ACTIVE or PENDING status”. It then asks whether this customer can fail one: a card the customer froze earlier, or one a freeze step of the same job left FROZEN, can. So it searches for a procedure whose result meets the requirement, finds unfreezing (“Confirm the card is now active”), reads that procedure's own requirements (FROZEN, account OPEN), and writes the unfreeze before the close.
  2. The rubric's backstop (D10). If the merge still closes a card the job can meet FROZEN, D10 sends it back. D10 knows no tool by name: it parses every document's requirement section and the steps section after it, and builds the table below. From that table it reads that the close needs ACTIVE or PENDING, and that the documents' tool for a FROZEN card leaves it ACTIVE. Its send-back cites both documents.
